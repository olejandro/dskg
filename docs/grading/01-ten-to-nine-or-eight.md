# Tenth to Ninth/Eighth Kyu

## Kihon (Basics)

- From zenkutsu-dachi (front stance), gedan-barai (downward block)
	1. Chudan oizuki (middle level front punch) → (Move forward, 5 times)
	2. Chudan gyakuzuki (middle level reverse punch) → (Move forward, 5 times)
	3. Jodan age-uke (head level rising block) → (Move forward, 5 times)
	4. Chudan soto-uke (middle level outer block) → (Move forward, 5 times)
- From zenkutsu-dachi (front stance)
	- Maegeri (front kick) → (Move forward, 5 times)
- From kiba-dachi (horse-riding stance)
	- Yokogeri-keage (side snap kick) → (3 times)
